<div class="alert note">

If you need strong data isolation and manage only a small number of tenants, you can create a separate collection for each tenant.

However, you can create a maximum of 16,384 collections depending on your [project plan and cluster deployment option](limits.md). Therefore, for large-scale multi-tenancy, consider alternative strategies such as partition-based or partition-key-based multi-tenancy, depending on your use case. For details, see [Implement Multi-tenancy](multi-tenancy.md).
