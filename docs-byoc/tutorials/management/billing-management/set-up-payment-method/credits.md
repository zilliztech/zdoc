# Credits

Credits can be used to cover Zilliz Cloud usage charges. You may receive credits when you register for Zilliz Cloud with a work email, participate in eligible Zilliz Cloud programs or events, or receive credits from Zilliz for a proof of concept (PoC).

Credits help you try Zilliz Cloud or run evaluation workloads before setting up a long-term payment method.

<Admonition type="info" icon="📘" title="Note">

To manage credits and payment method, you must be an **Organization Owner** or **Organization Billing Admin**.

</Admonition>

## How credits work\{#how-credits-work}

Credits are applied automatically to usage charges before other [payment methods](./payment-billing#payment-methods).

If multiple payment methods or balances are available, Zilliz Cloud applies them in the following order:

1. Credits

1. Advance Pay balance

1. Marketplace subscription

## Credit validity\{#credit-validity}

Credits have an expiration date. Expired credits cannot be used to cover future usage charges.

To avoid losing available credits:

- Check your remaining credit balance regularly.

- Check the number of days before your credits expire.

- Use credits before they expire.

- Add a valid payment method to extend your credit validity from 30 days to 1 year.

- Contact [Zilliz Support](http://support.zilliz.com) or your account executive if you have questions about credit validity.

## Credits and payment methods\{#credits-and-payment-methods}

Credits can be used together with either Advance Pay balance, or a Marketplace subscription.

However, credits are not a replacement for a long-term payment method. If your credits run out or expire and no other valid payment method is available, your organization will lose access to advanced features and will become frozen.

To continue using Zilliz Cloud after credits are used, set up one of the following payment methods:

- [Advance Pay](./advance-pay)

- [AWS Marketplace subscription](./subscribe-on-aws-marketplace-private-offer)

## Apply for credits\{#apply-for-credits}

If you need more credits for PoC, please [contact sales](http://zilliz.com/contact-sales) or your account executive.
